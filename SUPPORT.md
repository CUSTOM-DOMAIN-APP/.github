- **Service status:** [status.customdomain.ai](https://status.customdomain.ai)
- **Account and billing:** connect@customdomain.ai
- **Talk to the team:** [book a call](https://customdomain.ai/book)
- **Product:** https://customdomain.ai · [sign up free](https://app.customdomain.ai/signup)
- **Security:** report vulnerabilities privately to security@customdomain.ai, never in a public issue. Policy: [app.customdomain.ai/security](https://app.customdomain.ai/security)
